Tale of Two Coins

There was a tale between two merchants trading off their livelyhoods from their inheritance

The two were best friends until the that came to make a deal, a contract inline with their kingdom

However,

The two merchant, friends as they are, had biger dreams then dealing with common folk around them,
and in order to achieve their goals, they must make a pledge to fidelity to a lord.
Racing against the clock of their compatriots, the two made haste toward their neighboring lord.
A deal this lord struck between these two friends.

One would draw a sword, one would draw a feather pen, and one will draw a coin.

With one friend missing between the two, they both stuck in awe at their decision, to abandon everyone, but themselves.

A tale was then told from this lord, if there are two sides to a coin, why is there a third side, hidden from boths view.
